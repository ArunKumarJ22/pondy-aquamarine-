Terms & Conditions

Last updated: October 2026

Bookings

A booking is confirmed once we acknowledge it and receive a 50% advance payment. The balance is payable on the day by UPI, Google Pay, PhonePe or cash.

Pricing

The shared group ride is ₹500 per adult and ₹250 per child above 5 years. Couple, private charter, sunrise and sunset experiences are priced on enquiry. Prices may change without notice.

Safety

  • Life jackets must be worn at all times on the boat.
  • Follow the instructions of the boat crew and guide.
  • No smoking or drinking on board.
  • Children must be accompanied by an adult.
  • We may refuse boarding to anyone whose behaviour endangers others.

Eco-responsible conduct

Please do not litter or disturb wildlife and mangroves.

Timings and itinerary

Rides run daily between 6:00 AM and 5:30 PM and last about 1 hour to 1 hour 10 minutes. Stops and routes may change with tide, weather and safety conditions.

Liability

Guests take part at their own risk, within the safety measures we provide. We are not responsible for loss of personal belongings.
