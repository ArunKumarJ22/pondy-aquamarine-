Cancellation Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Weather and safety

If conditions are unsafe, we contact you in advance and offer a free reschedule or a full refund.

Cancellation by guest

  • Please inform us as early as possible on WhatsApp.
  • Rescheduling to another date is welcome, subject to availability.
  • The 50% advance is generally non-refundable for cancellations made on the day of the ride; earlier cancellations are handled case by case — contact us.

Late arrival and no-shows

Please arrive 15 minutes before departure. Late arrivals may be moved to the next available slot.

Refunds

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method (UPI/bank) within a few working days.
