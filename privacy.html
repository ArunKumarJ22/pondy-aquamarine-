Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Pondy Aquamarine Boating ("we") respects your privacy. This website has no accounts, database or payment processing.

What we collect

Only what you choose to send us through the enquiry or booking forms: your name, phone/WhatsApp number, email, preferred date, group size and message. These forms open WhatsApp or your email app with the details pre-filled, so the information is sent directly from your device to us.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and confirm your booking
  • To contact you about schedule or weather changes

Sharing

We do not sell or share your details with third parties. Messages sent through WhatsApp or email are also subject to those providers' own policies.

Third-party services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and an embedded Google Map, which may set their own cookies or log your IP address.
