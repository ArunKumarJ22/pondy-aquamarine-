Why Choose Us

More than a boat ride

At Pondy Aquamarine Boating we don't just ferry you across the water — we introduce you to a landscape. Our guides grew up on this estuary and share its birds, its history and its quiet secrets with every guest.

Small boats, unhurried pacing and genuine local hospitality mean every ride feels personal. Whether it's your first visit to Puducherry or your fifth, you'll leave with stories worth retelling.

Our Story

Born on These Waters

We grew up along the Thengaithittu Estuary, watching fishermen slip into the mangroves at dawn and herons lift off the tide. For us this isn't a destination — it's home, and we wanted to share its quiet magic with the world.

Since 2018 our family-run crew has kept groups small, boats well maintained and every ride respectful of the ecosystem that sustains us. We carry guests, not plastic; we leave only ripples.
